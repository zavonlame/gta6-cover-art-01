Neon Vice turns a photo and a caption into GTA-poster multi-panel cover art. Pick one of four layouts — Hero, Split, Trio or Grid — load a photo into each panel (or one photo for all of them), crop and position each with its own zoom and pan, choose a colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Night or Palm Dusk), and write the title and caption in neon-glow lettering over a procedural synthwave backdrop of a striped sun, a perspective grid, stars and palm silhouettes. Export the finished cover as a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline from static files, and the two sample photos in `app/samples/` are free to use.

Layouts: Hero is one photo front and centre; Split balances two panels side by side; Trio pairs one large panel with two stacked ones; Grid is four equal panels. Grades: Vice Sunset is warm pink and orange, Neon Night is cyan and magenta on dark blue, Palm Dusk is teal and gold.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
